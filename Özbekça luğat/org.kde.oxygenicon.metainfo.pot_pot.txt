Oxygen Icons is a freedesktop.org compatible icon theme originally developed for the KDE Plasma desktop environment in combination with the Oxygen Style. It features smooth gradients, soft shadows, and a slightly glossy look. - Oxygen Icons — bu freedesktop.org bilan mos keladigan belgilar mavzusi bölib, u dastlab Oxygen uslubi bilan birgalikda KDE Plasma iş stoli muhiti uçun işlab çiqilgan. U silliq gradientlar, yumşoq soyalar va biroz yaltiroq körinişga ega.
