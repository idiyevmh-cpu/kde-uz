KCalc - KCalc
Scientific calculator - Ilmiy kalkulyator
KCalc has everything you would expect from a scientific calculator, plus: - KCalc ilmiy kalkulyatordan kutilgan barça imkoniyatlarga va quyidagilarga ega:
Trigonometric functions, logic operations and statistical calculations - Trigonometrik funksiyalar, mantiqiy amallar va statistik hisoblaşlar
A results stack which enables convenient recall of previous calculation results - Oldingi hisob-kitob natijalarini qulay çaqirib oliş imkonini beruvçi natijalar steki
Precision is user-definable - Aniqlik darajasi foydalanuvçi tomonidan belgilanadi
The display allows cut and paste of numbers - Displey raqamlarni qirqib oliş va qöyiş imkonini beradi
The display colors and font are configurable, aiding usability - Displey ranglari va şrifti qulaylik yaratiş uçun sozlanadi
The use of key-bindings make it easy to use without a pointing device - Tezkor tugmalar yordamida siçqonçasiz ham oson foydalaniş mumkin
