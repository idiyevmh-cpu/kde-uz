Critical battery level - Kritik batareya darajasi
The battery charge will be considered critical when it drops to this level. After a brief warning, the system will automatically suspend or shut down, according to the configured critical battery level action. - Batareya quvvati uşbu darajaga tuşganda kritik deb hisoblanadi.
