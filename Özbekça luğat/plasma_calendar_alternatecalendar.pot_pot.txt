Calendar system: - Taqvim tizimi:
This calendar is based on pure astronomical calculation. It doesn't consider any crescent visibility criteria. - Uşbu taqvim sof astronomik hisob-kitoblarga asoslangan. Unda hilol (yangi oy) körinişi mezonlari hisobga olinmaydi.
Date offset: - Sana siljişi:
%1 day - %1 kun
A positive offset signifies a later date, while a negative offset signifies an earlier date. - Musbat siljiş keyingi sanani, manfiy siljiş esa oldingi sanani bildiradi.
