Window focus policy: - Oyna fokusi siyosati:
Click to focus - Fokuslaş uçun bosiş
Window becomes focused when clicked. - Oyna bosilganda fokusga ega böladi.
Focus follows pointer - Fokus körsatkiç ortidan ergaşadi
As the pointer moves, windows under it become focused after a configurable delay. - Körsatkiç harakatlanganda, uning ostidagi oynalar sozlanadigan keçikişdan keyin fokusga ega böladi.
Focus under pointer - Fokus körsatkiç ostida
Window under pointer receives focus, even if the pointer has not moved. - Körsatkiç harakatlanmagan bölsa ham, uning ostidagi oyna fokusni qabul qiladi.
Selecting <interface>Focus under pointer</interface> will prevent focus on some windows appearing on screen. This can lead to assigning focus to unwanted windows. Use with caution. - <interface>Fokus körsatkiç ostida</interface> tanlanganda ekranda paydo böladigan baʼzi oynalarga fokus berilişi töxtatiladi. Bu keraksiz oynalarga fokus berilişiga olib kelişi mumkin. Ehtiyotkorlik bilan foydalaning.
