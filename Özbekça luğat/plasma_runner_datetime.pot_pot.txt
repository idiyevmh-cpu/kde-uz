<time> <timezone> in <timezone> - <vaqt> <vaqt_mintaqasi> <vaqt_mintaqasi> da
Converts the time from the first timezone to the second timezone. If only one time zone is given, the other will be the system time zone. If no date or time is given, it will be the current date and time. - Vaqtni birinçi vaqt mintaqasidan ikkinçisiga ötkazadi.
