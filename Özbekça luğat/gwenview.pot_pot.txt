Defines what happens when going to image B after             having zoomed in on an area of image A.  If set to Autofit,             image B is zoomed out to fit the screen.  If set to KeepSame,             all images share the same zoom and position: image B is set             to the same zoom parameters as image A (and if these are             changed, image A will then be displayed with the updated zoom             and position).  If set to Individual, all images remember             their own zoom and position: image B is initially set to the             same zoom parameters as image A, but will then remember its             own zoom and position (if these are changed, image A will NOT             be displayed with the updated zoom and position). - A tasvirning biror qismini kattalaştirgandan söng B tasvirga ötilganda nima sodir bölişini belgilaydi. Agar Autofit (Avtomatik moslaştiriş) örnatilgan bölsa, B tasvir ekranga moslaşiş uçun kiçraytiriladi. Agar KeepSame (Bir xil saqlaş) örnatilgan bölsa, barça tasvirlar bir xil massştab va joylaşuvga ega böladi: B tasvir A tasvir bilan bir xil massştab parametrlariga örnatiladi (va agar ular özgartirilsa, A tasvir keyin yangilangan massştab va joylaşuv bilan körsatiladi). Agar Individual (Alohida) örnatilgan bölsa, barça tasvirlar öz massştabi va joylaşuvini eslab qoladi: B tasvir dastlab A tasvir bilan bir xil massştab parametrlariga örnatiladi, ammo keyin öz massştabi va joylaşuvini eslab qoladi (agar ular özgartirilsa, A tasvir yangilangan massştab va joylaşuv bilan KÖRSATILMAYDI).
Defines how colors are rendered when your display             uses an ICC color profile and an image has colors that do not             fit within the profile's color gamut. "Perceptual" will scale             the colors of the entire image so that they all fit within the             display's capabilities. "Relative" will squash only the colors             that cannot be displayed, and leave the other colors alone. - Displeyingiz ICC rang profilidan foydalanganda va tasvirda profilning rang qamroviga mos kelmaydigan ranglar bölganda ranglar qanday uzatilişini belgilaydi. "Perceptual" (Pertseptual) butun tasvir ranglarini displey imkoniyatlariga mos keladigan tarzda massştablaydi. "Relative" (Nisbiy) faqat körsatib bölmaydigan ranglarni siqib çiqaradi va boşqa ranglarni öz holiça qoldiradi.
