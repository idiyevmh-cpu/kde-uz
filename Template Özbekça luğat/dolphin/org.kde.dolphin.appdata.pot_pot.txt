Dolphin - Dolphin
Manage your files - Fayllaringizni boşqaring
Dolphin is KDE's file manager that lets you navigate and browse the contents of your hard drives, USB sticks, SD cards, and more. Creating, moving, or deleting files and folders is simple and fast. - Dolphin — bu qattiq disklar, USB fleşkalar, SD kartalar va boşqa vositalar tarkibini köriş imkonini beruvçi KDE fayl boşqaruvçisi. Fayllar va jildlarni yaratiş, köçiriş yoki öçiriş oson hamda tez bajariladi.
Dolphin contains plenty of productivity features that will save you time. The multiple tabs and split view features allow navigating multiple folders at the same time, and you can easily drag and drop files between views to move or copy them. Dolphin's right-click menu provides with many quick actions that let you compress, share, and duplicate files, among many other things. You can also add your own custom actions. - Dolphin vaqtingizni tejaydigan köplab qulayliklarni öz içiga olgan. Bir neçta içki varaqlar (tab) va oynani bölib köriş funksiyalari bir vaqtning özida bir neçta jildlar böyiça harakatlaniş imkonini beradi hamda siz fayllarni nusxalaş yoki köçiriş uçun sudrab ötirişingiz mumkin. Siçqonçaning öng tugma menyusi fayllarni siqiş, ulaşiş, nusxalaş kabi tezkor amallarni taqdim etadi. Şuningdek, öz amallaringizni ham qöşişingiz mumkin.
Dolphin is very lightweight, but at the same time, you can adapt it to your specific needs. This means that you can carry out your file management exactly the way you want to. Dolphin supports three different view modes: a classic grid view of all the files, a more detailed view, and a tree view. You can also configure most of Dolphin's behavior. - Dolphin juda yengil işlaydi, ammo şu bilan birga uni ehtiyojlaringizga moslab olişingiz mumkin. Bu esa fayllarni özingiz xohlagandek boşqarişingizni anglatadi. Dolphin uç xil köriniş rejimini qöllab-quvvatlaydi: klassik katakçali köriniş, batafsil jadval körinişi va daraxtsimon köriniş.
Dolphin can display files and folders from many Internet cloud services and other remote machines as if they were right there on your desktop. - Dolphin internetdagi bulut xizmatlari va boşqa masofaviy kompyuterlardagi fayllarni xuddi öz kompyuteringizdadek körsata oladi.
Dolphin also comes with an integrated terminal that allows you to run commands on the current folder. You can extend the capabilities of Dolphin even further with powerful plugins to adapt it to your workflow. You can use the git integration plugin to interact with git repositories, or the Nextcloud plugin to synchronize your files online, and much more. - Şuningdek, Dolphin joriy jildda buyruqlarni bajariş imkonini beruvçi örnatilgan terminalga ega. Git plaginlari orqali repozitoriylar bilan işlaş yoki Nextcloud plagini orqali fayllarni onlayn sinxronlaş imkoniyatlari mavjud.
