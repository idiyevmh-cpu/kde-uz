Anti-Aliasing: - Tekislaş (Anti-aliasing):
Enable - Yoqiş
Pixels on displays are generally aligned in a grid. Therefore shapes of fonts that do not align with this grid will look blocky and wrong unless <emphasis>anti-aliasing</emphasis> techniques are used to reduce this effect. You generally want to keep this option enabled unless it causes problems. - Displeylardagi piksellar odatda katak şaklida tekislanadi. Şuning uçun bu katakka töğri kelmaydigan şrift şakllari bu taʼsirni kamaytiriş uçun <emphasis>tekislaş (anti-aliasing)</emphasis> usullari qöllanilmasa, ğadir-budur va notöğri körinadi. Agar muammo tuğdirmasa, uşbu parametrni yoqilgan holda qoldirişingiz tavsiya etiladi.

Sub-pixel rendering: - Sub-piksel renderlaş:
<para>On TFT or LCD screens every single pixel is actually composed of three or four smaller monochrome lights. These <emphasis>sub-pixels</emphasis> can be changed independently to further improve the quality of displayed fonts.</para> <para>The rendering quality is only improved if the selection matches the manner in which the sub-pixels of your display are aligned. Most displays have a linear ordering of <emphasis>RGB</emphasis> sub-pixels, some have <emphasis>BGR</emphasis> and some exotic orderings are not supported by this feature.</para>This does not work with CRT monitors. - <para>TFT yoki LCD ekranlarda har bir piksel aslida uç yoki törtta kiçikroq monoxrom çiroqlardan iborat böladi. Körsatiladigan şriftlar sifatini yanada yaxşilaş uçun bu <emphasis>sub-piksellar</emphasis> mustaqil ravişda özgartirilişi mumkin.</para> <para>Renderlaş sifati faqat tanlov displeyingizning sub-piksellari tekislangan usuliga mos kelgandagina yaxşilanadi. Aksariyat displeylar <emphasis>RGB</emphasis> sub-piksellarining çiziqli tartibiga ega, baʼzilarida <emphasis>BGR</emphasis> mavjud, baʼzi ğayritabiiy tartiblar esa uşbu xususiyat tomonidan qöllab-quvvatlanmaydi.</para>Bu CRT monitorlarda işlamaydi.
Hinting: - Xinting (Hinting):
Hinting is a technique in which hints embedded in a font are used to enhance the rendering quality especially at small sizes. Stronger hinting generally leads to sharper edges but the small letters will less closely resemble their shape at big sizes. - Xinting — bu, ayniqsa, kiçik ölçamlarda renderlaş sifatini oşiriş uçun şriftga kiritilgan maslahatlardan foydalaniladigan usuldir. Kuçliroq xinting odatda aniqroq qirralarga olib keladi, lekin kiçik harflar katta ölçamdagi şakliga kamroq öxşaş böladi.
