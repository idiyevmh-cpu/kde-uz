Okular - Okular
View and annotate documents - Hujjatlarni köriş va ularga izohlar kiritiş
Okular is a universal document viewer developed by KDE. Okular works on multiple platforms, including but not limited to Linux, Windows, macOS, *BSD, etc. - Okular — bu KDE tomonidan yaratilgan universal hujjat köriş dasturi. Okular Linux, Windows, macOS, *BSD va boşqa köplab platformalarda işlaydi.
Features: - Xususiyatlari:
Supported Formats: PDF, PS, Tiff, DjVu, Images, DVI, XPS, Fiction Book, Comic Book, Plucker, EPub, Fax - Qöllab-quvvatlanadigan formatlar: PDF, PS, Tiff, DjVu, Rasmlar, DVI, XPS, Fiction Book, Comic Book, Plucker, EPub, Fax
Sidebar with contents, thumbnails, reviews and bookmarks - Mundarija, eskizlar, şarhlar va xatçöplarga ega yon panel
Annotations support - Izohlar (annotsiyalar) kiritişni qöllab-quvvatlaş
Reading manual in Okular - Okular dasturida qöllanmani öqiş
