Selection and Clipboard: - Tanlov va almaşiş buferi:
When text or an area of the screen is highlighted with the mouse or keyboard, this is the <emphasis>selection</emphasis>. It can be pasted using the middle mouse button.<nl/><nl/>If the selection is explicitly copied using a <interface>Copy</interface> or <interface>Cut</interface> action, it is saved to the <emphasis>clipboard</emphasis>. It can be pasted using a <interface>Paste</interface> action.<nl/><nl/>When turned on, this option keeps the selection and the clipboard the same,  so that any selection is immediately available to paste by any means. If it is turned off, the selection may still be saved in the clipboard history (subject to the option below), but it can only be pasted using the middle mouse button. - Matn yoki ekranning biror qismi siçqonça yoxud klaviatura yordamida belgilanganda, bu <emphasis>tanlov</emphasis> hisoblanadi. Uni siçqonçaning örta tugmasi yordamida joylaştiriş mumkin.<nl/><nl/>Agar tanlov <interface>Nusxa oliş</interface> yoki <interface>Qirqib oliş</interface> amali yordamida aniq nusxalansa, u <emphasis>almaşiş buferi</emphasis>ga saqlanadi. Uni <interface>Joylaştiriş</interface> amali yordamida joylaştiriş mumkin.<nl/><nl/>Bu parametr yoqilganda, tanlov va almaşiş buferi bir xil saqlanadi, şuning uçun har qanday tanlovni darhol istalgan usul bilan joylaştiriş mumkin böladi. Agar u öçirilgan bölsa, tanlov hali ham almaşiş buferi tarixida saqlanişi mumkin (quyidagi parametrga boğliq holda), lekin uni faqat siçqonçaning örta tugmasi yordamida joylaştiriş mumkin böladi.
