Gwenview - Gwenview
Image Viewer - Rasmlarni köruvçi
Gwenview is a fast and easy to use image viewer by KDE, ideal for browsing and displaying a collection of images. - Gwenview — bu KDE tomonidan yaratilgan tezkor va qulay rasm köruvçi dastur bölib, rasmlar töplamini varaqlaş va köriş uçun juda qulay.
Features: - Xususiyatlari:
Simple image editing: rotate, crop, resize, mirror, flip, reduce red eye, and adjust brightness, contrast, or gamma - Rasmlarni oddiy tahrirlaş: aylantiriş, qirqiş, ölçamini özgartiriş, akslantiriş, töntariş, qizil köz effektini yöqotiş hamda yorqinlik, kontrast va gammani sozlaş
Annotate and mark up images with arrows, shapes, text boxes, image stamps, and more - Strelkalar, şakllar, matnli bloklar, muhrlar va boşqalar yordamida rasmlarga izoh qöşiş
Basic file management actions such as copy, move, delete, and others - Nusxalaş, köçiriş, öçiriş kabi asosiy fayl boşqaruvi amallari
Main application window displaying an image and controls on the left - Asosiy ilova oynasida rasm va çap tomonda boşqaruv vositalari aks etgan
Use Gwenview to manage your image collection - Rasmlar töplamingizni boşqariş uçun Gwenview dasturidan foydalaning
