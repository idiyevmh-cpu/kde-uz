Icon Explorer - Nişonçalar tadqiqotçisi
Icon Previewer for Artists and Developers - Dizaynerlar va dasturçilar uçun nişonçalarni oldindan köruvçi
Icon Explorer allows artists and developers to preview and pick icons. Icon Explorer attempts to render the icons exactly as they would look drawn in the application. It allows to filter based on category and name. Icon Explorer is not a general-purpose tool, but geared towards artists' and developers' use-cases. Icon Explorer also provides a plugin for Kate and KDevelop to be used as icon picker. - Nişonçalar tadqiqotçisi dizaynerlar va dasturçilarga nişonçalarni oldindan köriş va tanlaş imkonini beradi. U toifasi va nomi böyiça filtrlaş imkonini beradi.
